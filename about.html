About ORIAWater · Heat · Time

The Philosophy of ORIA

A destination built around the oldest idea in wellbeing: warm water, held heat, and the time to let them work.

The name

Two Latin words, one place.

ORIA joins the edge where land meets water with the atmosphere a place gives off.

ōraLatin

The shore, the edge, the boundary of a place — where land meets water.

auraLatin

The air, the breath, the atmosphere that surrounds a place and stays with you.

Water · Heat · Time

Three elements, one rhythm.

01

Water

Mineral immersion and thermal rituals. Warm water, taken slowly — the body prepared, not hurried.

02

Heat

Warmth, contrast and relaxation. Dry heat and vapour, broken by cold, then released.

03

Time

Slowing down, and making space for restoration. Between the rituals, nothing is scheduled.

Beyond the traditional spa

More Than a Spa

ORIA is planned to bring thermal bathing together with longevity, recovery, movement, nutrition and mindfulness — one connected experience rather than a menu of treatments.

The Great Thermae

The Heart of ORIA

At the centre of the plan, a mineral pool beneath a stone dome. A single oculus lets daylight move across the water, so the room changes through the day.

The thermal ritual

Hot. Cold. Calm. Repeat.

An ancient sequence, taken slowly — two or three times across a day.

  1. 01Warm Springsmineral water, taken slowly
  2. 02Sauna & Steamdry heat and vapour, held
  3. 03Cold Plungebrief, bracing, deliberate
  4. 04Restheated stone, nothing asked of you
  5. 05Repeatthe sequence, not the room
